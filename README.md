# Songbird Pallium single-nuclei RNA and spatial transcriptomics
Cellular and spatial transcriptomics of several regions across the zebra finch pallium.
Providing a transcriptomic atlas of both evolutionarily novel and conserved brain regions of the bird pallium (dorsal telencephalon), spanning areas analogous to the mammalian motor, premotor, prefrontal, secondary auditory, and primary visual cortices. We focused on identifying potential cellular innovations in song-learning brain regions that underlie the eponymous vocal imitation behavior
